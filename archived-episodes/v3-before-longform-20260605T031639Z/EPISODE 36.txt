We had not reached the snow yet, but the hall knew its own next lesson. The air cooled as if embarrassed to be anticipated.

Sela hugged herself.

Noctis training made even wanting warmth complicated.

Tovin, or the impression of Tovin, whispered from a middle row:

Tovin: "Warmth costs."

Pella's echo answered from somewhere near the wall:

Pella: "Warmth is counted."

Iven, from the doorway, said in a voice that nearly failed:

Iven: "Not always."

He lifted Cael's cracked thermal charm.

The classroom shuddered.

Cael's name did not appear on the board.

That restraint was its own wound.

Elira's eyes found mine.

We both understood why. The hall wanted Cael as debt. It wanted his final act to become a lesson in owing pain to the dead. It could not have him that way if we refused to summon him as proof.

Sela looked at the charm.

Then at Mira.

Sela: "I don't know."

Mira nodded as if that were a complete answer.

Mira: "Then we can wait."

The hall hated that most of all.

The teacher's desk collapsed into black frost.

Lucien's reflection vanished from the desktops and reappeared in the window, now standing behind a field of white.

Lucien: "Very well. Let them learn the cost of waiting."

Before the snow crossed the threshold, the classroom made one final attempt to preserve itself.

It called roll.

Not by name.

By usefulness.

**RESERVE ELEVEN.**

Sela flinched but did not answer.

Mira's hand tightened on her notebook.

Mira: "Sela Mornet is present by name."

The classroom bell rang once.

No one moved.

**GREEN OBSERVER.**

Mira's crest flickered.

Finn said:

Finn: "Mira Castellan is present by name."

His voice cracked on her surname. It still worked.

**TACTICAL SUPPORT.**

Aira raised her eyebrows.

Aira: "Aira Solen is present and annoyed by lazy categorization."

The desks nearest her slid back.

**BINDER.**

Nera looked at her rings.

Darian took one breath beside her.

Nera: "Nera Vail is present. Binding is a skill, not a name."

The chalk scratched harder.

**DEFECTOR.**

Darian smiled faintly.

Darian: "Darian Thorne is present. Defection is a direction, not a person."

The hall hesitated.

It had expected shame.

It received grammar.

**BELOVED PRESSURE POINT.**

Elira's face went very still.

The whole room seemed to lean toward her, eager for either blush or fury, either of which it would have counted as evidence.

She gave it neither.

Elira: "Elira Vale is present. Beloved is a relationship I choose. Pressure point is a hostile use of someone else's fear."

The pendant pulsed once.

Clear.

**UNCLASSIFIED RESPONSE.**

Every reflection turned toward the place where my image stood in the classroom mirror though my body remained outside.

The old cold behind my sternum tightened.

I did not answer fast enough.

That delay mattered.

I wanted to reject the label perfectly. I wanted the sentence that would prove I understood everything we had survived. I wanted to be beyond the old traps, and that wanting was itself a trap with polished teeth.

Aira's voice came through the slate from inside:

Aira: "Haru."

Not Black Circle.

Not safeguard.

Not response.

Haru.

I breathed.

Haru: "Haru Aerlen is present outside the hall by witness condition."

The mirror cracked but did not break.

Lucien watched from the window.

Lucien: "Always outside until someone needs impossible power. Convenient."

Elira looked at his reflection.

Elira: "He is outside because we chose a structure where he is not the first tool used."

Lucien: "And when the first tools fail?"

Leon answered before she did.

Leon: "Then we use the next one without pretending the tool is a king."

Cassian: "Or a throne."

The word throne had not yet been spoken by Regalia.

Still, the classroom went cold at the sound.

Valen's voice entered through the record slate, distant but sharp.

Valen: "Note that response. The hall recognizes sovereign vocabulary."

Rhea: "Recorded."

The blackboard erased every label at once.

For half a second, it was blank.

Then a child's handwriting appeared in the lower corner.

Small.

Uneven.

Almost hidden behind the teacher's desk.

**I am cold.**

No name.

No route.

No dramatic plea.

A condition.

Mira saw it first.

She did not rush to read it aloud.

She pointed.

Aira followed her finger and went pale.

Aira: "Condition noted. No content demanded."

The classroom shuddered.

It had accidentally taught us the next rule.

Elira turned to Sela.

Elira: "Is that yours?"

Sela looked at the writing for a long time.

Sela: "Maybe."

Elira: "Maybe is accepted."

The word accepted moved through the children like warmth had begun before heat arrived.

Lucien's reflection thinned.

Lucien: "You will freeze under the weight of all these maybes."

Mira hugged her notebook to her chest.

Mira: "Then we will write them down before they melt."

Then the classroom floor became snow.

The change did not happen all at once.

First the ink froze in Mira's notebook.

Then the desks became drifts.

Then the Green row vanished under white, and every student who had been seated there found themselves standing with bare feet on ground that remembered winter drills.

The witnesses remained where they had chosen to stand, but the hall tried to reinterpret those choices as placements. Aira beside Mira became handler. Leon near the water-thread became resource officer. Cassian near the heat became ration keeper. Nera near the wall became binder again. Elira near Sela became authorized Yellow intervention.

One by one, they rejected the translations.

Aira: "Beside does not mean handler."

Leon: "Resource does not mean ownership."

Cassian: "Heat does not mean ration."

Nera: "Binding skill does not mean custody."

Elira: "Intervention does not outrank consent."

The snow paused around their ankles, as if recalculating.

Lucien's reflection watched from the white window.

Lucien: "You can rename positions forever and still not warm them."

Elira looked toward the students whose lips had already begun to pale.

Elira: "Correct."

The answer surprised him.

It surprised me too.

Elira: "Language is not warmth. But without correct language, warmth becomes debt before it reaches the skin."

Mira held her frozen notebook against her chest.

Mira: "Then we start with language and move quickly."

Aira gave a sharp nod.

Aira: "That is the least stupid summary of our lives."

The snow accepted the insult badly.

Good.

Inside the record slate, the field widened until it was too large for the classroom that had held it. The moving hall had changed lessons. It had stopped asking whether names could be recovered and begun asking what those names would cost once bodies needed help.

Outside, in the annex, Iven began to shake.

Maelin wrapped another blanket around him.

Iven: "They make you wait until someone offers heat. If you ask, you lose heat tomorrow. If you do not ask, they say you were strong."

Valen wrote with a face like grief carved into discipline.

Valen: "Winter endurance doctrine."

Iven: "No. Winter gratitude."

Nera closed her eyes inside the slate image as if the phrase had crossed distance to strike her.

Iven: "They call it gratitude."

The field heard him and grew colder.

That meant the hall was connected to his memory as well as theirs.

Rhea noticed at the same time.

Rhea: "Iven, you may stop warning us."

He clutched Cael's charm.

Iven: "No."

Rhea: "That no is accepted."

The charm glowed.

Only then did the snow field fully open.

By then, the witnesses had learned enough to be frightened properly.

Improper fear rushed, grabbed, erased, and called the wreckage rescue.

Proper fear counted exits. It asked who had been named, who had not, who was watching, who was being watched, who had warmth, who could refuse warmth, and who might be punished for admitting they needed it.

Aira wrote while walking.

I had no idea how.

Her pencil moved across paper stiff with cold, listing conditions faster than the hall could convert them into commands.

Aira: "Visible students: Sela Mornet confirmed by name. Tovin Reiss possible. Pella unknown. Josen unknown. Others unknown. Environmental pressure: cold, kneeling expectation probable, gratitude-debt language active."

Mira looked at her.

Mira: "How are you calm?"

Aira: "I am not. I am organized."

That difference became another small light in the field.

The hall tried to blow it out by sending wind across the page.

Finn stepped beside Aira and held his notebook up as a shield.

Finn: "Paper support."

Aira: "Deeply heroic."

Finn: "I am freezing."

Aira: "Still heroic."

Mira laughed once.

So did Sela, though the sound was barely there.

The snow field did not understand laughter that did not deny fear.

For a moment, the cold lost its rhythm.

That moment became the entry point.

Elira saw it and did not waste it.

Elira: "Sela, we are going to ask about warmth next. You do not have to be ready."

Sela stared at her.

Sela: "What if I say yes because I am cold?"

Elira: "Then we treat cold as a condition, not coercion by itself. We check whether punishment is attached. We make the warmth slow enough to refuse after it starts."

Sela: "You can refuse after yes?"

The snow field went very quiet.

There it was: another stolen thing.

Not the right to say no first.

The right to change yes.

Aira's voice softened.

Aira: "Yes can stop."

Finn wrote it.

Mira repeated it for the Green row.

Mira: "Yes can stop."

Nera repeated it for Noctis.

Nera: "Yes can stop."

The words moved from witness to witness until even Cassian said them, awkward and sincere.

Cassian: "Yes can stop."

Outside the hall, Iven began crying again.

This time, he did not apologize.

The cold lost its rhythm completely.

Lucien's reflection flickered in the window, and for the smallest instant his irritation became visible. He had built so many traps around refusal that he had forgotten consent also required the power to end agreement.

Elira noticed.

Of course she did.

Elira: "That is what you never learned."

Lucien: "I learned that hesitation costs lives."

Elira: "No. You learned to call control certainty because you were afraid people would leave if they could stop."

The reflection sharpened with anger.

Then the snow rose.

It rose around ankles first, then knees, then the hems of uniforms that had never been designed for winter fields. The hall did not freeze everyone equally. That would have been too honest. Green students received deeper snow. Blue students received instructions about distribution. Red students received heat they were not allowed to share without permission. Yellow students received clean paths and the shame of noticing them.

Elira stepped off her clean path.

The hall wrote **WASTED ADVANTAGE**.

She looked at Sela.

Elira: "No. Shared condition."

The clean path vanished.

Its disappearance did not make Elira safer. It made the lesson honest enough to fight. She stood in the same snow as Sela, not to perform humility, but because a path that only protected power was another kind of cage.

Sela saw the path disappear and understood before anyone explained it.

Sela: "You can be cold too?"

Elira: "Yes."

Sela: "But you could choose not to."

Elira: "That is why choosing matters."

The lesson had no answer for that except more cold.

That was answer enough.

For now, it would have to be.

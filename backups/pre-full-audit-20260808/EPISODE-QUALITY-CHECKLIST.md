# Episode Quality Checklist

Use before calling any future episode done.

## Structure

- Set-piece type chosen: hearing/political, physical/magical, intimate two-hander, or world/systems scene.
- Previous episode set-piece checked; avoid repeating the same type twice unless deliberately justified.
- Previous episode's final 500 words and current episode's first 500 words read together; no repeated aftermath, restarted arrival, or unmarked rewind.
- Previous three episodes checked; no more than one hearing-shaped centerpiece unless the arc explicitly demands consecutive proceedings.
- Episode shape chosen:
  - Beat: 5,000-7,000 words, focused breather or texture episode.
  - Standard: 8,000-10,000 words, default story movement.
  - Climax: 13,000-16,000 words, reserved for true arc turns.
- At least one live mystery advanced: Black Circle, Noctis, or Eris/Fifth Tower.
- If any mystery has gone untouched for 8+ episodes, add a pressure beat or deliberately defer it in notes.
- Any announced tournament, trial, journey, or public event receives an explicit completion, suspension, transformation, or abandonment on-page.
- No scene duplicates another scene's setup, question, answer, and emotional outcome; deliberate echoes must change at least two of those four elements.

## Prose Audit

- Count `That was` reveal-line openers; keep roughly one per scene.
- Count standalone one-word fragments; keep them for real pressure beats, not routine observation.
- Search `something`; replace with a specific noun when Haru or the narration actually knows what it is.
- Cut narration that restates what dialogue already proved.
- Search production words (`arc`, `episode`, `plot`, `reader`) and remove any that are not literal in-world language.
- Search recurring canon names/ranks against `CONTINUITY-BIBLE.md`; provisional or corrected names must be framed on-page.
- Vary paragraph architecture: include at least one scene every few episodes with sustained sensory description instead of dialogue -> reaction -> fragment rhythm.
- Check short-paragraph ratio and paragraph-leading `Not`, `No`, and `That was`; revise routine instances until fragments mark pressure instead of becoming default cadence.
- Keep ordinary-location metaphor distinct from literal enchanted response; if a room "answers," the reader should know whether magic actually moved.

## Character And Agency

- Haru makes or refuses an action with visible cost; restraint cannot be only passive withholding.
- Across every 2-3 episode movement, Haru chooses a proactive objective rather than only answering a test, filing, attack, or institutional schedule.
- Elira has agency beyond being cared for; if she uses major power, give the body/recovery cost real weight.
- At least one character outside Haru, Elira, and Aira gets a real beat that changes pressure, not just a check-in line.
- Leon, Cassian, Mira, Iven, or another secondary character solves at least one problem through action when the episode is physical.
- Lucien, Krail, Sovan, or Noctis pressure changes shape instead of repeating the same hearing/motion pattern.

## Rule And Continuity Gate

- Any Black Circle rule, cost, limit, or exception is earned on-page.
- Any Elira medical/recovery change is consistent with prior cost.
- Any Fifth Tower mechanic follows the seeded rules or clearly establishes a new one.
- Dialogue tag style remains deliberate: `Name: "Line"` by default, with action beats added when emotional clarity needs them.
- Any POV switch receives a named heading before the first-person pronouns change.
- Episode opening time/place agrees with the previous episode's closing scene and coda.
- `CONTINUITY-BIBLE.md` updated after drafting.
- `STORY-SPINE.md` consulted before opening or closing major mysteries.

## Fifth Tower Arc Specifics

- Do not solve the tower through another hearing.
- Use physical space, routes, pressure, and consequences.
- Keep Green witness access central without making Green students props.
- If Haru uses the Black Circle, establish a visible cost in the same scene.
- If Elira unleashes Yellow Circle power, make it powerful, chosen, and medically consequential.

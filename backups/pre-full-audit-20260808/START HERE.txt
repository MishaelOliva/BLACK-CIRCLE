# **BLACK CIRCLE: THE QUIETEST POWER**

**World Primer · 00**

---

Every magician is born with a rank they did not choose.

The **Magic Circle** awakens near the heart during childhood. When magic is channeled, its visible crest can surface at the wrist, throat, collarbone, or another natural casting point. Wherever it appears, its color determines the shape of a life before that life has had the chance to decide anything.

Everyone is taught that a Circle's color cannot change.

**Green Circle** magicians make up roughly sixty percent of the population. They maintain wards, route transit systems, and perform the necessary work that everyone depends on and almost no one names. Green is the foundation of civilization. It is also the easiest rank to dismiss.

**Blue Circle** magicians make up roughly thirty percent. Skilled and respected, they move through institutions with fewer doors closed against them. The system does not place them at the top, but it was built to recognize their value.

**Red Circle** magicians make up roughly nine percent. They become heirs, council officers, commanders, and institutional leaders. When they enter a room, the room adjusts.

**Yellow Circle** magicians are the remaining one percent: the highest rank human magic has officially produced. They are admired, feared, recruited, and never left alone.

Beyond Yellow, the accepted knowledge stops.

What lies beyond is called **Black Circle** in old texts—a celestial rank so far past Yellow that comparison loses meaning. No verified public record proves that a living bearer has ever existed. Most scholars dismiss it as mythology. Most institutions prefer to.

---

**Haru Aerlen** transfers into **Aurelis Arcane Academy** mid-year with a flawless Green crest and a careful plan to stay invisible.

The plan is good.

The crest is false.

His true Circle is Black.

Aurelis is beautiful, competitive, and cruel in the precise way elite institutions can be cruel: through traditions no one is supposed to question and rules that sound neutral until they close around the powerless. Rank decides who receives patience and who becomes an example. Rumors do damage before anyone confirms them.

Then **Elira Vale**, one of Aurelis's most formidable Yellow Circle students, treats Haru like a person before she treats him like a rank. Friendship makes invisibility harder. The approaching inter-academy tournament makes it dangerous. And the students of **Noctis Dominion** begin noticing that impossible things keep surviving around Aurelis's quiet Green transfer.

Haru's problem is no longer simply that he might be discovered.

It is that the people around him are real.

His greatest conflict is not whether he has power. It is whether he can keep choosing restraint when the institution closes its fist around the people he cares about, when the tournament turns protection into spectacle, and when every act of mercy gives the world another piece of evidence against him.

---

True strength does not stand above others.

It protects them.

# Episode 22: **The Boy Beneath the School**

The tunnel had opened only after the annex stopped mistaking command for courage.

By then we knew three things: Iven Sahl was alive below us, the route punished urgency, and every step had to be chosen slowly enough for the old magic to believe us.

We entered the service tunnel without formation because fear moved faster than command.

Dren corrected that with one word.

Dren: "Line."

Everyone snapped into place. Not because he shouted.

Because the tunnel punished haste.

The stone ceiling pressed low enough that Cassian had to duck. Old pipes ran along the walls, carrying condensation that tasted of iron and mint. The academy above us sounded far away, like a memory trying to pretend it was not responsible for the basement.

Iven Sahl's blue-white trace pulsed ahead. Small. Weak. Alive.

Nera walked like a person whose guilt had become a compass. Darian stayed close but did not touch her unless she asked. That restraint mattered in a tunnel built by people who had never understood the difference between support and control.

Elira's pendant glowed beneath her collar.

Every few steps, it asked a silent question.

Every few steps, she answered.

Yes. I choose to continue.

Yes. I understand the risk. No. Haru is not carrying me. No. Fear is not consent.

The questions brushed against me too, not as accusations but as structure. I had thought I would hate that.

I did.

I also needed it.

The service tunnel ended at a round door with no handle.

Leon crouched near the floor.

Leon: "Water behind it."

Cassian: "A flood trap?"

Leon: "No. A listening trap. The water repeats footsteps."

Aira leaned over him.

Aira: "Can you make it repeat the wrong ones?"

Leon looked offended.

Leon: "Yes."

Cassian smiled.

Cassian: "He loves when we ask him to be clever."

Leon: "I love silence more."

He placed both hands against the stone. Blue light spread from his fingers into the thin moisture seam under the door. The water trembled, then began repeating our last forty steps backward.

From inside the room, a mechanism clicked.

The door opened away from us.

The chamber beyond was not large.

That made it worse. One chair. One table.

One old tournament relay mirror turned to face the wall.

One boy on the floor with his wrists bound in white cord.

Iven Sahl looked younger than I remembered.

At Cael's funeral, distance and grief had made him part of a group. Here, curled on cold stone with a gag spell trembling over his mouth, he was only a first-year student with damp hair, tear tracks, and a Blue Circle crest flickering so weakly it almost went gray.

Nera made a sound that broke.

The gag spell brightened.

Iven's eyes widened in terror.

Elira lifted one hand.

Gold light warmed the room without touching him.

Elira: "Iven. We are Aurelis witnesses. Nera is here. Darian is here. You are not alone."

The gag spell pulsed.

The relay mirror turned. Slowly. No one touched it.

In its surface, Lucien appeared seated in a white Noctis chamber with a cup of tea in one hand.

Lucien: "How tender."

Dren raised his blade.

Halden said, "Wait."

Dren's blade stopped a hair from the mirror.

Halden's eyes were on the white cord around Iven's wrists.

Halden: "The mirror is secondary. The restraint is primary."

Lucien: "Professor Halden remains useful. How unfortunate for him."

Iven began shaking his head.

The gag prevented speech, but not meaning.

No. Don't. Leave.

Nera stepped forward.

Lucien's eyes moved to her reflection.

Lucien: "Miss Vail. You continue to disappoint everyone who improved you."

Nera flinched.

Darian's hand lifted, then stopped.

Nera saw it. She breathed.

Nera: "No. Everyone who used me."

Her binding rings opened. Lucien smiled.

The white cords around Iven's wrists flashed black.

Nera froze.

I understood too late.

The trap was not for me.

It was for anyone trying to prove they were no longer Noctis.

The cords spoke in old Dominion grammar:

**RETURN REQUIRES A HAND THAT BELONGED TO US**

Nera's rings began pulling toward Iven's wrists.

If she touched the cord, it would name her as Noctis property returning another Noctis asset.

Darian moved in her place.

The cord flashed again.

**DESERTER HAND INVALID**

Darian stopped with murder in his eyes.

Lucien drank tea.

Lucien: "The problem with sentimental reform is that institutions remember paperwork better than feelings."

I looked at the cord.

The Black Circle could erase it.

It would also erase whatever fragile route held Iven's wrists intact.

Elira crouched slowly, outside the cord's reach.

Maelin cursed but did not stop her.

Elira: "Iven. Can you move your left fingers?"

The boy stared at her.

Elira: "Not for us. For you. If you can."

One finger twitched.

The cord tightened.

Iven's face twisted.

The Black Circle surged.

Aira said, "Haru." One word. Not warning. Witness. I held.

Elira's gold light narrowed to a thread and touched the floor beside Iven's hand, not the cord.

Elira: "Good. Again, if you choose."

Another finger moved. Then another.

Mira whispered, "He is answering himself."

Finn wrote it down with shaking hands.

Aira's eyes lit.

Aira: "The cord requires a Noctis hand. It did not say the hand had to be outside the restraint."

Lucien's cup paused. There. The crack.

Rhea stepped forward.

Rhea: "Iven Sahl, under Aurelis emergency witness authority and Inter-National student protection protocol, your own hand is recognized as sufficient answer to any claim made on your body."

The cord tightened again.

Iven cried soundlessly.

Maelin snapped, "Pain spike."

Elira's hand clenched but her voice stayed steady.

Elira: "Iven. You do not have to be brave for us. You only have to be present for one second at a time."

Nera knelt several feet away, tears on her face.

Nera: "Cael lent you a thermal charm because you were cold."

Iven's eyes moved to her.

Nera: "He told me afterward. He said Noctis made children stand in snow and called it discipline. He hated that."

The boy's crest flickered blue.

Nera: "He would hate this."

The gag spell trembled.

Darian's voice was rough.

Darian: "You are not a reserve."

Iven's left hand opened. Not much.

Enough.

The white cord asked for a Noctis hand.

Iven gave it his own.

The first loop loosened.

Lucien set down the cup.

Lucien: "Stop."

That single word carried more truth than his speeches.

Everyone heard it.

The second loop loosened.

Then the gag spell changed.

It slid from Iven's mouth toward his throat. No longer silence. Punishment. I moved.

This time, I did not ask permission because there was no time for the kind of permission a dying body could not give.

But I did not seize the whole room.

I placed the Black Circle between the gag and Iven's airway.

A boundary. A refusal.

The spell struck it and shattered into black dust.

Iven screamed.

The sound filled the chamber, raw and terrified and alive.

Maelin was already moving. She slid beside him, not touching the cord, one hand above his chest.

Maelin: "Breathing. Shallow. Pulse fast but present."

Elira kept the floor thread steady.

Nera held both hands up where Iven could see them.

Nera: "I will not touch you unless you ask."

Iven sobbed.

Iven: "Don't send me back."

Nera broke completely then.

Nera: "Never."

The last cord fell open.

The relay mirror cracked.

Lucien's image stood.

Behind him, for the first time, I saw the edge of a real room: white walls, black relay lattice, and shadows of students behind glass.

More reserves.

Iven saw them too.

His eyes went blank with panic.

Elira turned to the mirror.

Gold light rose around her, low and controlled.

Elira: "We have your room now."

Lucien looked at her.

For the first time since he had entered our lives, his calm did not fully return.

Lucien: "No. You have a glimpse."

Halden stepped beside her.

Halden: "A glimpse with geometry."

Aira: "And witness notes."

Mira lifted her notebook.

Mira: "And boring verbs."

The mirror fracture widened.

Lucien's eyes moved from Elira to me.

Lucien: "You keep letting them stand between us."

Haru: "Yes."

Lucien: "Afraid?"

Haru: "Learning."

He hated that. Not visibly enough for most people.

Enough for me.

The mirror shattered outward into harmless light.

The chamber returned to stone, cord, breath, and a rescued boy who shook so badly Maelin ordered everyone but Nera, Darian, Elira, and me to step back.

Iven looked at me from the floor.

Fear entered his face again. Not of Noctis. Of me. That mattered too.

I lowered myself slowly until I sat on the cold stone several feet away. No magic. Hands visible.

Haru: "You do not have to trust me."

Iven swallowed.

His voice came out broken.

Iven: "Are you the Black Circle?"

The chamber listened.

For months, I had survived by hiding from that question.

Now a frightened child asked it from the floor of a hidden room built to make children useful.

I answered.

Haru: "Yes."

Iven stared at me.

Iven: "Are you going to take us?"

The Black Circle did not move.

My heart did.

Haru: "No."

Elira sat beside me, close enough that our shoulders almost touched.

Elira: "He is going to help us make sure no one does."

Iven looked at the crystal, then at Nera, then at Maelin.

Iven: "Cael said Aurelis students were weird."

Nera laughed and cried at the same time.

Cassian, from the doorway, said, "Accurate."

Leon: "Painfully."

Iven's mouth trembled. Not quite a smile.

But not only fear.

Above us, academy bells began ringing emergency shelter sequence.

Rhea listened, then went pale.

Rhea: "That is not our bell order."

The old annex lights turned red.

Senna's voice burst through every ward channel at once.

Senna: "All schools alert. Noctis Dominion College has sealed itself from Council entry. Multiple student signatures moving below registered ground. Lucien Voss has activated an internal doctrine site."

Iven closed his eyes.

Iven: "The obedience hall."

Nera's hands stopped at her sides.

Darian whispered a curse.

I looked at Elira.

The honeymoon was gone.

The world had entered the room.

But when she took my hand, it was not as a shield.

It was as a promise that what we had chosen in private would survive being tested in public.

Elira: "Then we find the hall."

Iven opened his eyes.

Iven: "You can't."

His voice shook.

Iven: "It moves."

---

Maelin refused to let the sentence become strategy until Iven had water.

That should have irritated everyone.

It saved everyone.

The room wanted to become a war council around a child still shaking on the floor. Maelin made it become a treatment room first. She ordered Cassian to warm the air without visible flame, Leon to pull damp from the stone, Aira to stop writing directly beside Iven's head, and me to sit where the boy could see both my hands.

I obeyed.

That seemed to offend Leon more than if I had argued.

Leon: "He listens to healers now."

Cassian: "Growth terrifies you."

Leon: "It destabilizes my worldview."

Iven stared between them like he could not decide whether Aurelis students were safe or insane.

Elira sat on the floor near him, not close enough to crowd, close enough to be chosen if he needed a face that had also survived a doctrine using her body as a route.

Nera remained by the wall.

That hurt her.

She did it anyway.

Iven kept looking at her, then away, then back again.

Finally he whispered, "You were in the blue corridor."

Nera's face changed. Not guilt alone. Recognition.

Nera: "Yes."

Iven: "You checked our rings."

Nera closed her eyes for half a breath.

Darian looked at the floor.

Nera: "Yes."

Iven's voice shook harder.

Iven: "You loosened mine once."

No one found a first movement.

Nera opened her eyes. Iven swallowed.

Iven: "After winter drills. When Magister Owel said I was wasting heat. You adjusted the ring. It hurt less after."

Nera's hand covered her mouth. No dramatic apology could have survived that moment.

She did not try one.

Nera: "I should have done more."

Iven looked confused by the answer. Not because it was wrong.

Because Noctis had taught him that small mercies were either accidents or debts.

Elira spoke softly.

Elira: "Both can be true. She helped once. She should have been able to help more. Noctis made more dangerous than it should have been."

Iven stared at her.

Iven: "Are you allowed to say that?"

Elira's smile was small and sharp.

Elira: "I died. It made me less polite."

Aira made a sound into her sleeve.

Maelin pointed at her without looking.

Maelin: "Do not encourage the patient."

Elira: "I am encouraged internally."

Maelin: "I know. It is medically obvious."

Iven's mouth twitched. Not a full smile. Still a victory.

Rhea entered after sealing the outer tunnel. Her council robe was dusty at the hem and her expression had passed beyond irritation into something colder.

Rhea: "The academy shelter order has been corrected. Students are contained in assigned halls. Seraphine and Dren have the annex perimeter. Senna is trying to keep Krail out of the channel."

Aira: "How successful?"

Rhea: "Senna just used the phrase jurisdictional vandalism."

Aira's eyes lit.

Rhea: "No."

Aira looked wounded.

Rhea turned to Iven.

Her voice changed. Less president. More witness.

Rhea: "Iven Sahl, I need to ask about the obedience hall. You can refuse. You can stop. You can ask for Maelin, Nera, Darian, Elira, or no one near you."

Iven clutched the blanket around his shoulders.

Iven: "If I say no, do you send me somewhere else?"

Rhea: "No."

He waited, like one no could be a trick and the second might show the hook.

Rhea stayed silent.

At last he nodded.

Iven: "Ask."

Rhea: "Why does it move?"

Iven looked toward the broken mirror.

Iven: "Because they said fear is portable."

The room hated that sentence.

Iven continued before hatred could interrupt.

Iven: "The hall appears where a student expects punishment. Sometimes under Noctis. Sometimes in dormitory glass. Sometimes in the training field. Sometimes in a classroom after someone important smiles too kindly."

Mira, from the doorway, wrote with shaking hands.

Finn stood beside her, lips moving as he silently counted clauses.

Iven: "It knows the shape you think discipline will take."

Haru: "And if someone is not afraid?"

Iven looked at me like I had asked whether the lake was not wet.

Iven: "Everyone is afraid."

No one corrected him.

Because he was right enough.

Iven's gaze dropped to his hands.

Iven: "It moves through people who learned to wait for it."

Nera whispered, "Conditioned anticipatory route."

Halden, who had returned to the threshold with Valen, nodded once.

Halden: "A doctrine site anchored through expectation."

Valen: "That is why standard location seals fail."

Darian: "Can we draw it here?"

The question was ugly. Necessary.

Iven went white.

Darian immediately stepped back.

Darian: "No. I am sorry. Not through you."

Iven's breathing hitched.

Maelin moved closer.

Iven shook his head.

Iven: "It will come anyway."

Elira leaned forward.

Elira: "Why?"

He looked at me.

Then at her pendant.

Iven: "Because Lucien wants to show the hall what open rings look like."

The crystal warmed.

Elira's hand rose to it.

Iven: "He said Aurelis would try to make weakness look like mercy. He said open rings are how weak people pretend not to belong to stronger ones."

Mira's Green Circle flickered at her wrist.

Anger. Not power.

Something better in that moment.

Iven: "If open rings enter the hall, it tries to close them. If it closes them around someone strong enough..."

His eyes returned to me.

I understood.

Haru: "It makes a Black Circle image."

Iven nodded.

The room shifted around the answer.

Lucien had built the next public proof before we arrived.

If Haru entered, the hall would become a visual accusation.

If Elira entered with the revised pendant, it would try to collapse her open consent lines into my symbol.

If no one entered, children remained inside.

Cruelty loved triangles with no clean side.

Aira closed her notebook.

The sound was small. Final.

Aira: "Then Haru does not enter first."

Everyone looked at her.

Aira's face was pale but set.

Aira: "Do not make that expression at me. I hate this too."

Elira: "Who does?"

Mira stepped from the doorway. Not far.

Enough.

Mira: "Someone it thinks is weak."

Finn whispered, "Mira." Mira swallowed.

Mira: "Not alone. Not untrained. Not as sacrifice. But if the hall is built from hierarchy, maybe a Green Circle witness can go somewhere a Black Circle cannot."

Iven stared at her like she had just proposed walking into winter without permission to be cold.

Mira looked terrified.

She did not take it back.

The old annex lights continued pulsing red.

The obedience hall moved somewhere beyond the walls.

And for the first time, our answer did not begin with my power.

---

The obedience hall moved because Noctis had built it out of fear instead of stone.

Iven explained from a healer cot in the old annex recovery room while Maelin replaced every restraint in sight with blankets, water, and visible exits. The boy kept looking at the door as if it might become a Noctis instructor if he forgot to watch it.

Nera sat near his feet. Not too close. Close enough.

Darian stood by the wall with his arms folded, silent and furious. Rhea had turned the outer corridor into a sealed witness station. Aira, Mira, and Finn occupied the table with notes, maps, registry fragments, and the terrifying orderliness of people who had realized paperwork could become a rescue weapon if sharpened correctly.

Elira sat beside me on the floor because there were not enough chairs and because Maelin had finally decided that sitting counted as obedience.

Iven: "It is not always under Noctis."

His voice was small but steadying.

Iven: "Sometimes it is a room. Sometimes it is a corridor. Sometimes it is the training field after lights out. The seniors say only weak students think it moves. But it does."

Halden: "A portable doctrine site."

Valen: "Or a site anchored to student fear signatures instead of location."

Iven nodded too quickly, then flinched at his own agreement.

Elira: "Take your time."

He looked at her.

Whatever he saw in her face helped.

Iven: "They make conduit-track students stand in the hall when someone disobeys. You hear everyone who failed before you. Not voices exactly. More like... instructions wearing voices."

Nera closed her eyes.

Nera: "Return when called. Kneel when named. Spend when chosen."

Iven's face crumpled.

Nera opened her eyes immediately.

Nera: "Sorry."

Iven shook his head.

Iven: "No. That's it."

Mira wrote the words down, then underlined them so hard the paper tore.

Aira gently slid her a second sheet.

Rhea entered with Senna's remote seal floating beside her shoulder.

Senna looked more tired than I had ever seen her.

Senna: "The Council cannot enter Noctis. The institution sealed itself under sovereign educational emergency doctrine, claiming outside contamination by Black Circle influence."

Leon: "They are using Haru as a lock."

Senna: "Yes."

Krail's petition had become a key for Lucien.

Not because Krail served Lucien directly. That would have been simpler.

Because fear rarely cared who fed it.

Rhea: "Can the Council break the seal?"

Senna: "Legally, eventually. Magically, not before the missing students are moved or spent."

The room understood spent. No one said Cael's name.

We did not need to.

Elira's ward warmed.

Iven stared at it.

Iven: "That is the thing he used."

Every eye turned.

Elira's hand stilled at her throat.

Iven swallowed.

Iven: "Not that. Sorry. Not yours. The shape. Open rings. They taught us closed rings are loyal. Open rings are weakness. Then Lucien made us practice breaking open rings because he said Aurelis would use them to pretend mercy was strength."

Valen whispered, "He studied Witness Restoration."

Halden: "He studied how to corrupt it."

Aira: "And how to make students afraid of the countermeasure before they ever saw it."

Iven nodded.

Iven: "If you bring open-ring magic near the obedience hall, it changes shape."

Elira: "Into what?"

Iven's eyes moved to me.

Iven: "A black circle."

The room went silent. Not because the answer was surprising.

Because it was too precise.

Lucien had spent months building a public argument that Black Circle proximity contaminated witnesses. Now he had a hidden hall trained to make open witness magic collapse visually into my symbol.

If we tried to rescue the missing students, he could make the rescue look like proof.

Krail would not even need to lie.

He would only need to describe the image.

I stood.

Elira stood with me despite Maelin's look.

Haru: "Then I cannot go."

The words hurt less than I expected because they were obvious.

They hurt more because Elira did not immediately argue.

She looked at me for a long moment.

Then nodded.

Elira: "Not into the hall."

The Black Circle pressed against my ribs.

Haru: "You are not going alone."

Elira: "No."

She turned to the room.

Elira: "We send witnesses the hall has been trained to underestimate."

Mira looked up. Then down.

Then up again.

Mira: "Green Circle?"

Elira: "Green Circle. Blue support. Former Noctis witnesses. People whose magic cannot be easily reframed as Haru's dominance."

Rhea: "Absolutely not."

Mira's face fell. Rhea continued.

Rhea: "Not without training, legal seals, extraction routes, and a way for Haru to support from outside without touching the hall."

Mira blinked.

Aira grinned slowly.

Aira: "President Valmont, was that an absolutely not or an aggressively conditional yes?"

Rhea: "It was a stop smiling before I assign you forms."

Aira stopped smiling. Mostly.

The plan formed in pieces.

Elira would lead the outer witness pattern because the hall expected her pendant and had been trained to corrupt its image. She would not enter the core. Nera and Darian would identify Noctis doctrine markers. Leon would manage water mirrors to carry sound without reflection. Cassian would maintain heat breaks to stop frost routes. Mira and Finn would carry Green Circle witness logic into the first threshold, not as fighters but as record anchors.

I would remain in the lower observatory with Halden, Valen, Rhea's remote seal, and Dren.

Outside the hall.

Close enough to answer if everything failed.

Far enough that my presence could not be used as the first proof.

I hated it.

Therefore it was probably correct.

Training began before sunset.

The academy's lower practice court had once hosted tournament selection drills. Now it became a rescue rehearsal under witness conditions. Dren set moving obstacles. Leon flooded shallow channels across the floor. Cassian burned frost marks out of the air with his left hand while the right remained wrapped. Aira shouted corrections with the enthusiasm of someone who had discovered fear could be organized.

Mira failed the first threshold test.

The open ring around her Green Circle collapsed inward the moment Halden simulated Noctis accusation pressure.

She went pale.

Mira: "Sorry."

Halden: "Do not apologize to a test."

Mira looked startled.

Halden: "Again."

She failed again.

Then a third time.

On the fourth, Finn stepped beside her and read from her own notes.

Finn: "A Green Circle witness does not prove power by winning. A Green Circle witness proves sequence by remaining countable."

Mira laughed once, panicked and breathless.

Mira: "I wrote that?"

Finn: "In very intense handwriting."

She tried again.

The ring stayed open.

Across the court, Elira trained without high-output casting. Her gold light remained thin, precise, and infuriatingly elegant. She moved slower than before the Wounded City, but every step was chosen. Maelin watched like a storm cloud with a medical license.

At one point Elira stumbled.

I moved half a step.

She saw it.

So did everyone else.

She steadied herself on the practice rail. Not on me.

The Black Circle scraped against my restraint like a blade against bone.

Elira looked at me from across the court.

Elira: "I am all right."

Haru: "I know."

Both statements were partly true.

That counted. Night came.

The moving hall found us before we found it.

It began as a bell. Not the academy emergency bell.

A Noctis training chime, soft and precise.

Iven screamed from the healer station.

Every open ring in the practice court turned toward the west wall.

Stone melted into a corridor of black glass.

At its far end stood twelve students in white Noctis sleep uniforms, barefoot, eyes open, hands at their sides.

Iven pushed himself upright.

Iven: "Those are them."

The students spoke together.

"Return when called."

The corridor widened.

Behind the twelve, Lucien stood in darkness with one hand resting lightly on the shoulder of a little Green Circle girl I did not know.

The Black Circle went silent. Not calm. Silent.

Lucien: "You wanted the hall. Here it is."

He looked past Elira.

Past the witnesses. At me.

Lucien: "Stay outside, Haru Aerlen. Show them restraint. I wonder how many children your discipline can watch suffer."

The little girl's face did not move.

But one tear fell.

Mira stepped forward.

Her Green Circle trembled. Then opened.

Mira: "We are coming in."

Lucien's smile turned curious.

The moving hall swallowed the first witnesses.

---

Inside the threshold, sound changed first.

Mira would tell us later that the practice court did not vanish all at once. It thinned. The walls became reflections. The reflections became windows. The windows became black corridor glass, and each pane showed a different version of herself failing before she had begun.

Mira dropping her notebook. Mira crying. Mira kneeling.

Mira apologizing for making everyone protect a Green Circle student when stronger people had real work to do.

The hall had found her fear quickly.

It had not found all of her.

Aira's voice reached her from the left.

Aira: "Do not believe any image where I am not visibly annoyed."

Mira almost laughed.

That almost mattered.

Elira entered behind them and the corridor light shifted gold for one second, then snapped back to black. The hall tested the crystal immediately. Seven open arcs appeared around the dark crystal at her throat, then tried to close inward like teeth.

Elira touched the pendant.

Elira: "No."

The arcs opened again. Not by force. By refusal.

Leon and Cassian entered together, which neither of them commented on because emergencies sometimes made dignity out of convenience. Leon sent a thin water line along the floor. Cassian warmed it from beneath so the water stayed moving instead of freezing into the hall's preferred shape.

Nera and Darian entered last.

The corridor hated them.

Noctis memory surfaced along the walls in white script.

DESERTER. FAILED BINDER. UNGRATEFUL HAND. WITNESS CONTAMINATED.

Darian looked at the words, then at Nera.

Darian: "Boring."

Nera blinked.

Darian: "They used better insults when we were loyal."

For one startled breath, Nera laughed.

The white script flickered.

The hall had been prepared for shame.

Not humor shared by people who knew the shame was real and still refused to worship it.

Outside, we heard only fragments through Valen's slate.

But the slate showed enough.

Mira standing too stiffly.

Aira at her shoulder, pen ready like a knife no one sensible would underestimate.

Elira behind them, luminous and visibly tired, refusing to let exhaustion become absence.

The black-glass corridor stretched ahead toward the little Green Circle girl under Lucien's hand.

The girl did not blink.

That was what convinced me she was fighting.

Children under full coercion often performed blankness too perfectly. This blankness had seams. Her left hand twitched every few seconds. Her mouth held still, but her jaw trembled near the hinge. Her Green Circle flickered at the edge, hidden under a white Noctis training cuff.

Mira saw it too.

Mira: "She is awake."

Lucien's voice drifted through the corridor.

Lucien: "Awake is not the same as free."

Mira swallowed.

Mira: "I know."

Lucien tilted his head.

He had expected denial.

Mira gave him record.

Aira whispered, "Good."

The first trap opened as a school bell. Not Aurelis. Noctis.

The sound was clean, high, and obedient. Every missing student in the corridor turned at once toward the far wall and placed both hands at their sides.

Mira's body moved too. Only a little.

Conditioning did not need to belong to Noctis to find a student. Every academy taught students to obey bells in some form. Lucien had chosen a sound that made schools complicit before anyone spoke.

Elira's eyes sharpened.

Elira: "No one moves by bell."

Leon: "By what, then?"

Elira looked at Mira.

Mira's face went pale.

Then she understood.

Mira: "By name."

The corridor floor cracked.

Lucien's smile thinned.

Mira opened her notebook with hands that shook badly enough to make the pages whisper.

Mira: "I am Mira Castellan."

Aira: "Aira Solen."

Elira: "Elira Vale."

Leon: "Leon Varek."

Cassian: "Cassian Veyr."

Nera: "Nera Vail."

Darian: "Darian Thorne."

The bell rang again.

This time, none of them moved.

The missing students trembled. Not freed. Invited.

The little Green Circle girl's eyes shifted toward Mira. Only her eyes.

Mira took one step.

Lucien's hand tightened on the girl's shoulder.

Outside, the Black Circle pressed against my ribs so hard I nearly lost the room.

Iven's voice came from the healer cot behind me.

Iven: "He does that when he wants the hall to think for you."

I turned my head.

Iven was sitting upright despite Maelin's objections, face white, blanket clenched in both hands.

Iven: "He touches one and everyone remembers being touched."

Valen adjusted the slate.

Rhea sent Iven's sentence into the record.

## Composition Rules

- Covers must fill the full frame with important faces and action centered away from crop edges.
- Scene images should read clearly at thumbnail size: one main moment, limited characters, readable emotion.
- Use environmental storytelling: council rails, witness circles, academy corridors, old annex rooms, Noctis frost relays, Regalia doors, infirmary chairs, record crystals.
- Do not use text inside the image except abstract unreadable magical glyphs.
- Intimacy scenes must be mature, romantic, and sensual but non-explicit: clothed or covered bodies, no exposed genitals, no graphic sex act, focus on faces, hands, pendant, candlelight, breath, closeness, trust.

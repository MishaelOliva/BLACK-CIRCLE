# Episode 22: **The Boy Beneath the School**

The tunnel had opened only after the annex stopped mistaking command for courage.

Dren took point with Leon and Cassian clearing the damp stone ahead, followed in measured file by Aira, Halden, Maelin, Rhea, Mira, Finn, Nera, and Darian, while Elira and I stepped down last from Cael's old recovery room, leaving Seraphine, Valen, and Pella to hold the upper annex gate and seals. By then we knew three things: Iven Sahl was alive below us, the route punished urgency, and every pace had to be chosen slowly enough for the old magic to believe us.

Fear still pushed the back of the line faster than formation.

Dren stopped it with one word.

Dren: "Line."

Everyone snapped into place, because the dark ahead tightened whenever anyone rushed.

The stone ceiling pressed low enough that Cassian had to duck, keeping his frost-bandaged right wrist tucked close to his ribs. Old pipes ran along both walls, slick with condensation that smelled of rust and bitter mint. Above us, the academy sounded distant, like a tidy lecture hall trying to disown its own basement.

Ahead, Iven Sahl's blue-white pulse beat through the damp air. Small. Weak. Alive.

Nera walked as if guilt had turned into a plumb line. Darian stayed half a pace behind her without reaching out unless she looked back. That restraint mattered in a corridor dug by people who had never learned the difference between support and custody.

Beneath Elira's collar, her pendant gave a soft, steady glow.

Every few steps, it asked a silent question. Every few steps, she answered it under her breath.

Yes. I choose to continue.

Yes. I understand the risk. Haru is not carrying me. Fear is not consent.

The questions brushed against my ribs too, less like accusation than counterweight. I hated needing the check. I needed it anyway.

The service tunnel dead-ended at a round iron door with no handle.

Leon crouched beside the threshold, palm hovering an inch above the damp stone.

Leon: "Water behind it."

Cassian: "A flood trap?"

Leon: "Listening trap. The water repeats footsteps."

Aira leaned over his shoulder.

Aira: "Can you make it repeat the wrong ones?"

Leon shot her an offended glance, then shifted his weight onto his knees. Cassian grinned in the dark.

Cassian: "He loves when we ask him to be clever."

Leon: "I love silence more."

He pressed both palms flat to the threshold. Pale blue light threaded from his fingers into the hairline moisture seam under the door. Behind the iron, the trapped water shivered, reversed its current, and began replaying our last forty footsteps backward toward the stair.

A latch clicked inside.

The round door swung away from us.

The room beyond was cramped, which made the sight inside worse. One wooden chair. One scarred table. An old tournament relay mirror turned toward the wall.

And one boy curled on the cold stone with his wrists bound in white cord.

Iven Sahl looked smaller than he had at Cael's funeral. There, distance and rows of Noctis coats had folded him into a group. Here, shivering with damp hair clinging to his forehead and a gag spell trembling across his mouth, he was only a first-year student whose Blue Circle crest flickered so faintly it bordered on gray.

Nera let out a broken breath.

At the sound, the gag spell flared brighter. Iven's eyes went wide with panic.

Elira raised one empty hand. Warm gold light spread across the floor without touching him.

Elira: "Iven. We are Aurelis witnesses. Nera is here. Darian is here. You are not alone."

The gag pulsed harder.

Across the room, the relay mirror rotated on its stand with a dry scrape of wood, though nobody stood near it.

Inside the glass, Lucien Voss sat in a white Noctis chamber with a porcelain teacup balanced in one palm.

Lucien: "How tender."

Dren drew his blade halfway across the threshold.

Halden: "Wait."

Dren froze with steel a hair from the glass. Halden's eyes stayed fixed on the white cord biting into Iven's wrists.

Halden: "The mirror is secondary. The restraint is primary."

Lucien: "Professor Halden remains observant. How inconvenient for him."

Iven began shaking his head hard against the floor. The gag stole his voice, not his meaning. *No. Don't. Leave.*

Nera stepped forward anyway. Lucien's gaze slid to her in the mirror.

Lucien: "Miss Vail. You continue to disappoint everyone who improved you."

Nera flinched. Darian's hand lifted half an inch, then stopped before it crossed into her space.

Nera saw the restraint, drew one sharp breath, and lifted her chin.

Nera: "Everyone who used me."

The binding rings at her wrists unlocked with a metallic snap. Lucien smiled.

At Iven's wrists, the white cords flashed pitch black. Nera froze, and I saw the snare a second too late.

The trap had never been built for me. It was built for anyone trying to prove they no longer belonged to Noctis.

White Dominion lettering flared along the cord:

**RETURN REQUIRES A HAND THAT BELONGED TO US**

Nera's rings jerked toward Iven's wrists on an invisible tether. If she touched that cord, the spell would log her as Noctis property reclaiming another Noctis asset.

Darian stepped in front of her and thrust his own hand toward the rope.

The cord flashed again.

**DESERTER HAND INVALID**

Darian halted with murder written plain across his face. In the mirror, Lucien took a slow sip of tea.

Lucien: "The trouble with sentimental reform is that institutions remember paperwork longer than feelings."

I stared at the white knots cutting into Iven's skin. The Black Circle could strip the spell away in half a breath. It would also shred whatever fragile circulation still kept the boy's hands intact.

Elira crouched slowly just outside the cord's reach. Maelin muttered a curse behind us, then stayed back.

Elira: "Iven. Can you move the fingers on your left hand?"

The boy stared up at her, chest heaving.

Elira: "Not for us. For you. Only if you can."

One finger twitched. The cord bit tighter, and Iven's face twisted in silent agony. Behind my sternum, the Black Circle surged like dark water hitting a lock.

Aira: "Haru."

Just my name. No lecture, no panic. I locked my shoulders and held.

Elira narrowed her gold light to a single hair-thin thread and laid it on the stone beside Iven's knuckles, leaving the cord untouched.

Elira: "Good. One more, if you choose."

A second finger uncurled. Then a third.

Mira: "He is answering himself."

Finn wrote it down on his pad with shaking hands. Aira's eyes sharpened.

Aira: "The cord demands a Noctis hand. It never specified a hand outside the knots."

In the mirror, Lucien's teacup paused halfway to the saucer. There was the seam.

Rhea stepped forward, council sigil flaring crimson at her sleeve.

Rhea: "Iven Sahl, under Aurelis emergency witness authority and Inter-National student protection protocol, your own hand is recognized as sufficient answer to any claim made on your body."

The cord cinched down harder. Iven arched silently against the stone.

Maelin: "Pain spike!"

Elira's free hand curled into a fist against her knee, though her voice stayed level.

Elira: "Iven. You do not have to be brave for us. Stay present for one second at a time."

Nera dropped to her knees several feet away, tears cutting tracks through the dust on her cheeks.

Nera: "Cael lent you a thermal charm because you were freezing."

Iven's gaze jerked to her face.

Nera: "He told me afterward. He said Noctis made children stand in snow and called it discipline. He hated that."

The boy's Blue Circle crest gave a ragged flicker.

Nera: "He would hate this."

The gag spell trembled against Iven's jaw. Beside Nera, Darian's voice came out rough as gravel.

Darian: "You are not a reserve."

Iven's left hand opened. Barely an inch, yet enough for his own fingertips to brush the white cord against his wrist.

The spell had asked for a Noctis hand. Iven gave it his own.

The first loop slackened and dropped away.

Lucien set his teacup down with a sharp click.

Lucien: "Stop."

That single syllable carried more truth than any speech he had ever given. The second loop fell loose.

Then the gag spell mutated. It peeled off Iven's mouth and slid down toward his throat, tightening into a strangle-band. No longer silence. Execution.

I moved without asking, because a boy losing his airway could not grant permission. Even then, I refused to let the power take the whole chamber. I drove a narrow wedge of Black Circle shadow strictly between the spell and Iven's throat, a blunt boundary, nothing more.

The gag struck it and blew apart into weightless black ash.

Iven dragged in air and screamed.

The sound tore through the cramped stone room, raw and terrified and unmistakably alive.

Maelin slid to his side before the echo died, hovering one palm above his chest without touching the fallen cord.

Maelin: "Airway clear. Breathing shallow. Pulse fast, steady."

Elira kept her gold thread resting quietly on the floor beside him. Nera held both open palms up where Iven could track them.

Nera: "I won't touch you unless you ask."

Iven choked on a sob.

Iven: "Don't send me back."

Nera's composure gave way completely.

Nera: "Never."

The last loop unraveled. Hairline fractures spidered across the relay mirror. In the glass, Lucien stood up.

Behind his chair, for half a second before the angle skewed, I caught the edge of a real room: white plaster walls, a lattice of black relays, and motionless silhouettes of students behind frosted glass. More reserves.

Iven saw them too. Panic hollowed out his eyes.

Elira turned to face the cracked glass, low gold light gathering along her sleeves without flaring.

Elira: "We have your room now."

Lucien met her gaze, and for the first time since his name entered our lives, his composure did not snap cleanly back into place.

Lucien: "You have a glimpse."

Halden stepped beside Elira.

Halden: "A glimpse with geometry."

Aira: "And witness notes."

Mira lifted her scribbled notebook so the glass caught it.

Mira: "And boring verbs."

The split down the mirror widened with a sound like cracking ice. Lucien's eyes shifted past Elira to me.

Lucien: "You keep letting them stand between us."

Haru: "I asked them to."

Lucien: "Afraid?"

Haru: "Learning."

He hated that answer. Most people wouldn't have caught the shift around his mouth, only a slight stiffening at the jaw. I caught it.

The mirror burst outward into harmless motes of silver light.

Silence rushed back into the stone cell, leaving only damp air, slack white cord, and a trembling boy so pale Maelin waved everyone except Nera, Darian, Elira, and me back toward the doorway.

Iven looked up at me from the floor, and fresh terror crossed his face. Terror of me, not Noctis.

I lowered myself slowly until I sat cross-legged on the cold stone six feet away, hands resting open on my knees.

Haru: "You don't have to trust me."

Iven swallowed hard.

Iven: "Are you the Black Circle?"

The room went dead quiet. For months I had survived by dodging that question in corridors and hearings. Now a bruised first-year asked it from the floor of a secret basement built to break children into tools.

Haru: "Yes."

I kept my eyes on Iven's without looking away. He stared back, breathing hard.

Iven: "Are you going to take us?"

The Black Circle stayed still beneath my skin. My pulse didn't.

Haru: "No."

Elira sat down beside me, close enough for our shoulders nearly to brush without crowding the boy.

Elira: "He is going to help us make sure nobody else does."

Iven looked at the dark crystal gleaming faintly against her collar, then at Nera by the wall, then at Maelin hovering with her healer kit.

Iven: "Cael said Aurelis students were weird."

Nera let out a laugh that broke straight into a sob. From the doorway, Cassian leaned his head in.

Cassian: "Accurate."

Leon: "Painfully."

Iven's mouth twitched, halfway between terror and disbelief.

Then the academy bells began ringing overhead, loud enough to vibrate down through the pipes. Emergency shelter sequence.

Rhea went rigid, listening to the cadence.

Rhea: "That isn't our bell order."

The damp bulbs along the ceiling bled crimson. Senna's voice tore through every ward channel in the room at once.

Senna: "All schools alert. Noctis Dominion College has sealed itself from Council entry. Multiple student signatures are moving below registered ground. Lucien Voss has activated an internal doctrine site."

Iven squeezed his eyes shut.

Iven: "The obedience hall."

Nera's hands went dead still at her sides. Darian swore under his breath. I looked at Elira. Whatever quiet the lake house had bought us ended right there, and the world walked straight into the basement.

She didn't reach for my hand as a shield. When her fingers closed briefly around mine, it felt like a promise that what we had chosen in private would hold under public weight.

Elira: "Then we find the hall."

Iven opened his eyes, shivering violently.

Iven: "You can't. It moves."

Dren sheathed his blade and headed back up the service stair with Halden at his shoulder to reinforce Seraphine on the upper annex gate, leaving the rest of us with Iven in the cellar.

---

Maelin refused to let anyone turn Iven's warning into a war council until the boy had drunk half a cup of water.

She cleared space around him like a field surgeon clearing shrapnel. Cassian was ordered to warm the air with his left hand without showing open flame, Leon to draw damp out of the stones under Iven's knees, Aira to stop scratching pencil notes right beside his ear, and me to sit where both my hands remained in plain sight. I obeyed without a word.

Leon stared at me as if I had personally insulted him.

Leon: "He listens to healers now."

Cassian: "Growth terrifies you."

Leon: "It ruins my filing system."

Iven looked back and forth between them, clearly trying to decide whether Aurelis students were safe or out of their minds.

Elira sat on the floor near the boy, leaving enough open stone between them that he could shift away if he wanted, close enough that he could see another survivor who knew what it felt like when a doctrine used a body as a conduit.

Nera stayed backed against the far wall. Every second there cost her, and she stayed anyway.

Iven's eyes kept darting toward her, away, and back again.

Iven: "You were in the blue corridor."

Recognition cut across Nera's face sharper than guilt.

Nera: "I was."

Iven: "You checked our rings."

Nera shut her eyes for half a beat while Darian stared grimly at the floor.

Nera: "I did."

Iven's voice shook harder.

Iven: "You loosened mine once."

Nobody moved. Nera opened her eyes.

Iven: "After winter drills. When Magister Owel said I was wasting heat. You adjusted the ring. It hurt less after."

Nera pressed both hands over her mouth. Any grand apology would have sounded obscene in that room, and she didn't insult him by offering one.

Nera: "I should have done more."

Iven blinked at her, bewildered, as if Noctis had trained him to believe small mercies were either mistakes or future invoices.

## Interlude: Elira - The Choice of Stride

Elira kept her voice low so it wouldn't corner him.

Elira: "Both can be true. She helped once, and she should have been able to help more. Noctis made even small mercies dangerous."

Iven stared at her.

Iven: "Are you allowed to say that?"

Elira's mouth curved, small and sharp.

Elira: "I died. It made me less polite."

Aira muffled a sound against her sleeve. Without looking up from Iven's pulse, Maelin pointed straight at the archivist.

Maelin: "Do not encourage the patient."

Elira: "I am encouraged internally."

Maelin: "I know. It shows in your chart."

Iven's lips twitched again, a fraction closer to a real smile. Elira eased her stiff left knee against the flagstone and refused to lean forward. Her ribs still ached when she breathed too deep, and the old Wounded City fatigue sat behind her eyes like lead, reminding her why stride mattered. If she moved too fast, her Yellow Circle would swallow the room and turn rescue back into hierarchy. She chose the slower pace on purpose.

Rhea stepped back down the tunnel stair after sealing the outer door, dusty hems sweeping the floor, her expression cold past irritation.

Rhea: "The academy shelter order has been corrected. Students are secured inside assigned halls. Seraphine and Dren have the annex perimeter, and Senna is working to keep Krail off the ward channel."

Aira: "How successfully?"

Rhea: "Senna just used the phrase jurisdictional vandalism."

Aira's eyes lit up.

Rhea: "No."

Aira slumped, wounded, while Rhea turned toward Iven. When the Student Council president spoke to the boy, the command edge dropped from her voice.

Rhea: "Iven Sahl, I need to ask about the obedience hall. You can refuse. You can stop whenever you want. You can ask for Maelin, Nera, Darian, me, or no one near you."

Iven clutched the wool blanket tighter around his shoulders.

Iven: "If I say no, do you send me somewhere else?"

Rhea: "Never."

He waited, watching her face as if the first promise were bait and the second might show the hook. Rhea simply waited with him until he gave a small nod.

Iven: "Ask."

Rhea: "Why does it move?"

Iven glanced at the shattered relay mirror.

Iven: "Because they said fear is portable."

Elira felt her jaw tighten, though she kept her hands still in her lap.

Iven: "The hall shows up wherever a student expects punishment. Under Noctis. In dormitory glass. On the training field. Sometimes in an ordinary classroom right after someone important smiles too kindly."

From the doorway, Mira scribbled furiously with trembling fingers while Finn's lips moved silently beside her, counting clauses.

Iven: "It learns the shape you think discipline will take."

Haru: "And if someone isn't afraid?"

Iven looked at Haru as if he had asked whether lake water was wet.

Iven: "Everyone is afraid."

Nobody argued, and Elira certainly didn't. Iven dropped his eyes to his bruised knuckles.

Iven: "It travels through people who learned to wait for it."

Nera: "Conditioned anticipatory route."

Halden, who had come back down the stair with Professor Valen at his elbow, gave one grim nod from the threshold.

Halden: "A doctrine site anchored through expectation."

Valen: "That explains why standard perimeter seals fail."

Darian: "Can we draw it here?"

The question was ugly and necessary. All color drained from Iven's face, and Darian took an immediate step back.

Darian: "No. I'm sorry. Not through you."

Iven's breathing hitched while Maelin shifted closer. He shook his head quickly.

Iven: "It will come anyway."

Elira leaned forward an inch, careful not to loom.

Elira: "Why?"

Iven's gaze flicked from Haru to the dark crystal resting against her throat.

Iven: "Because Lucien wants to show the hall what open rings look like."

The pendant warmed against Elira's skin, its seven open threads humming faint warning. She laid two fingers lightly beneath it without hiding it.

Iven: "He said Aurelis would try to pass weakness off as mercy. He said open rings are just how weak people pretend they don't belong to stronger ones. If open rings walk into the hall, it tries to snap them shut. And if it closes them around someone strong enough..."

His eyes landed back on Haru, and Elira understood the rest before Haru named it.

Haru: "It forces a Black Circle image."

Iven nodded.

Elira stared at the cold stone between her boots, tracing every angle of the trap Lucien had built ahead of them. If Haru stepped inside first, the hall would turn his shadow into public proof of Krail's panic. If she walked in ahead with the revised pendant, the room would fight to crush her seven open consent lines back into Haru's mark. And if nobody entered, children remained locked inside a moving cage.

Aira snapped her notebook shut. The small click cut cleanly through the room.

Aira: "Then Haru does not enter first."

Every head turned toward her. Aira looked pale, her chin set hard.

Aira: "Don't look at me like that. I hate this too."

Elira: "Then who leads?"

Mira stepped clear of the doorway, small and shaking, yet refusing to back up.

Mira: "Someone it expects to be weak."

Finn: "Mira."

Mira swallowed hard and kept her eyes on Elira and Rhea.

Mira: "Not alone, not untrained, and not as bait. But if the hall runs on rank and fear, maybe a Green Circle witness can cross a threshold a Black Circle can't."

Iven stared at her as though she had volunteered to walk barefoot into a blizzard without asking permission to shiver. Mira looked terrified down to her fingertips, and she didn't take a single word back.

Above them, the red annex bulbs continued pulsing against the wet pipes while the obedience hall shifted somewhere beyond the walls. Elira looked at Mira, then at Haru sitting quietly with empty hands, and let the pace settle where it belonged.

For the first time since the bells began, their answer did not start with Haru's power.

---

Senna's remote seal flared above Rhea's shoulder before anyone could turn Mira's offer into another committee meeting, her projected face gray with exhaustion and sharp enough to make the light crackle.

Senna: "Noctis just sealed itself under sovereign educational emergency doctrine. Official pretext: contamination by Black Circle influence."

Leon: "They're using Haru as the deadbolt."

Senna: "Precisely."

She slashed a dark ink mark across her margin. Krail's petition had handed Lucien a legal key without Krail ever needing to strike a deal.

Rhea: "Can you break the seal?"

Senna: "Legally, eventually. Magically, not before the missing students are relocated or spent."

Nobody spoke Cael's name, yet Iven's fists clenched white around his blanket.

Rhea swept her gaze across the cellar.

Rhea: "No repeated speeches. Decisions."

Mira held up her torn notes, pages trembling.

Mira: "I cross the first threshold."

Finn: "We cross."

Mira: "You don't have to make every bad choice hereditary."

Finn: "Good. Then this one is mine."

Nera reached toward the Noctis sigils Iven had sketched, paused the moment he flinched, and tapped the blank margin instead.

Nera: "I can tell original instructions from the lines Lucien rewrote. I can't promise I'll spot them before they spot me."

Darian: "Then I spot you."

Nera met his eyes. He didn't soften the pledge or dress it up.

Elira: "I hold the outer consent pattern. Leon runs sound without giving the glass a clean reflection. Cassian keeps the frost lines from locking shut."

Cassian raised his wrapped right wrist, then held up his left hand with a crooked grin.

Cassian: "One working hand, deeply offensive confidence, adequate fire."

Rhea: "And Haru stays outside."

Every person in the room waited for me to fight it. Part of me wanted to. The Black Circle had already offered fifty ways to turn the west wall into an empty hole, and not one of them ended with frightened children trusting whoever stepped through the dust.

Haru: "Close enough to catch failure. Far enough not to become Lucien's exhibit."

Elira looked at me steadily, resting two fingers lightly against the back of the wooden chair beside Iven's cot.

Elira: "Stay where we can still reach your voice."

It stung because she understood the cost down to the inch, and steadied me for the exact same reason.

Rhea: "Practice court. Now."

We climbed back up from the cellar into the cool air of the old tournament practice court while Maelin wheeled a portable cot to the far wall so Iven could stay clear of the stone floor. Dren met us at the court doors and shifted the stone barriers into a mock corridor before sunset bled out of the sky. Leon laid water in hair-thin channels along the paving stones while Cassian warmed them from beneath, grumbling that gentle heat was a crime against his element.

Mira failed the first threshold rehearsal.

Halden's simulated accusation struck her open ring, and the Green light folded inward as if ashamed of taking up air.

Mira: "Sorry."

Halden: "Don't apologize to a drill. Name what caught you."

She stared down at the dead circle on her wrist.

Mira: "It showed everyone shielding me while useful people died somewhere else."

Her second attempt collapsed faster. The third never opened at all.

Finn plucked the torn doctrine sheet out of her fingers and turned it face down without reading it.

Finn: "Mira Castellan. Copied forty-three witness statements when Council asked for one. Found two falsified timestamps. Abysmal handwriting."

Mira let out a startled laugh that caught in her throat.

Mira: "That's not in the notes."

Finn: "The notes were losing."

He pressed the paper back into her palm.

Finn: "Countable. Try again."

Her fourth ring trembled, held, and stayed open.

Across the court, Elira worked with a single slender thread of gold instead of the sweeping wards she used to throw without thinking. The Wounded City had left her slower, neither fragile nor fully mended, and every movement carried deliberate care.

Her boot toe caught on a raised paving seam.

I shifted half a step forward, then caught myself and stayed put.

Elira grabbed the court's barrier rail to steady herself, drawing one sharp breath through her teeth. When she glanced over at me, irritation arrived a full beat ahead of relief.

Elira: "Don't make me waste breath telling you I'm fine."

Haru: "I won't ask."

The irritation cracked open into real warmth.

Elira: "Thank you."

She meant thank you for stopping. I hated that stopping could be the hardest gift I knew how to give, and gave it anyway.

Night hadn't fully settled when the moving hall found us.

A clean Noctis training chime rang straight out of the solid west wall.

From the healer station, Iven screamed.

Every open crest on the practice court snapped toward the sound. Stone thinned and blackened into seamless mirror glass. Beyond it stretched a long corridor lined with twelve barefoot students in white Noctis sleep uniforms, eyes wide open, arms pinned neatly at their sides.

Iven dragged himself upright against Maelin's bracing hands.

Iven: "That's them."

The twelve spoke in dead unison.

Missing Students: "Return when called."

Behind their line, Lucien rested one calm hand on the shoulder of a little Green Circle girl I had never seen before.

The Black Circle inside my chest went utterly still.

Lucien: "You wanted the hall. Here it is."

He looked straight past Elira and every prepared witness, pinning his gaze on me.

Lucien: "Stay outside, Haru Aerlen. Show them restraint. I wonder how many children your discipline can watch suffer."

The little girl didn't blink, yet one slow tear traced down to her chin.

Before I could turn that tear into an excuse, Mira stepped forward. Her Green Circle trembled once, then flared steady.

Mira: "We're coming in."

Finn gripped his scroll case with both hands and stepped up half a pace behind her. Lucien's smile shifted from certainty to sharp curiosity.

The black-glass threshold parted and swallowed all eight witnesses, Mira and Finn leading Aira, Elira, Leon, Cassian, Nera, and Darian into the glare, before snapping thin behind them.

---

Inside the threshold, sound changed first.

Mira told us later the practice court didn't vanish at once; it diluted. Stone thinned into reflections, reflections into windowpanes, and windowpanes into a black-glass corridor where every surface replayed a different version of her failing before she opened her mouth.

Mira dropping her pad. Mira weeping. Mira kneeling. Mira apologizing for making stronger people bleed to guard a Green Circle desk.

The hall found her oldest fear in seconds, yet it hadn't measured all of her.

Aira: "Ignore any reflection where I don't look visibly annoyed."

Mira nearly laughed, and the tension across her shoulders broke just enough to breathe.

Elira crossed behind Finn, and for one heartbeat gold light swept the corridor before snapping back to obsidian. Seven open arcs flared around the dark crystal at her throat, then tried to snap shut like jaws. Elira laid one fingertip over the stone.

Elira: "No."

The arcs sprang wide again, refusing the lock.

Leon and Cassian entered shoulder to shoulder, too busy working to trade jokes. Leon ran a hair-thin thread of water along the floorboards while Cassian breathed low left-handed heat beneath it so the moisture kept moving instead of freezing into the hall's geometry.

Nera and Darian crossed last, and the walls hated them on sight.

White Noctis lettering bled straight out of the black glass:

**DESERTER. FAILED BINDER. UNGRATEFUL HAND. WITNESS CONTAMINATED.**

Darian read the wall, then glanced sideways at Nera.

Darian: "Boring."

Nera blinked at him.

Darian: "They had better insults when we still wore the uniforms."

A startled laugh tore out of Nera's throat, and the white script guttered like a candle in a draft. The hall had braced for shame, not two defectors who knew the shame was real and refused to kneel to it anyway.

Outside in the practice court, Dren, Halden, Maelin, Rhea, and I crowded around Valen's witness slate, catching fragmented images and strained audio through the failing glass.

It showed enough: Mira standing rigid in the front, Finn clutching his scroll case at her elbow, Aira at her shoulder with pencil poised like a blade, Elira behind them, pale and luminous, refusing to let exhaustion look like surrender.

Ahead of them stretched the corridor toward Lucien's hand on the little Green Circle girl.

She hadn't blinked once, yet her disguise had seams. Every few seconds her left fingers twitched, and the hinge of her jaw trembled beneath the forced stillness. Beneath a white Noctis training cuff, a faint Green Circle flickered.

Mira saw it first.

Mira: "She's awake."

Lucien's voice drifted smoothly down the glass hall.

Lucien: "Awake is hardly the same as free."

Mira swallowed hard.

Mira: "I know."

Lucien tilted his head, faintly surprised; he had baited her for denial and got plain record instead.

Aira: "Good."

The first trap struck as a school bell. Crisp, high, unmistakable Noctis brass.

Every missing child in the corridor snapped toward the far wall with palms slapped flat to their thighs. Even Mira's heels twitched an inch before she caught herself. Conditioning didn't have to carry a Noctis crest to find a student; every school taught bells before it taught names.

Elira's eyes sharpened.

Elira: "Nobody moves by bell."

Leon: "By what, then?"

Elira looked straight back at Mira. Mira went pale, then nodded once as the answer clicked.

Mira: "By name."

A hairline crack split the black floor. Lucien's smile thinned.

Mira cracked open her notebook with trembling fingers.

Mira: "I am Mira Castellan."

Finn: "Finn Halward."

Aira: "Aira Solen."

Elira: "Elira Vale."

Leon: "Leon Varek."

Cassian: "Cassian Veyr."

Nera: "Nera Vail."

Darian: "Darian Thorne."

The bell tolled a second time. None of them flinched.

Down the corridor, the twelve missing children shivered, invited even if not yet free. The little Green girl's eyes flicked toward Mira without turning her head. Mira took one careful step forward.

Lucien's fingers tightened on the girl's shoulder.

Outside by the slate, the Black Circle slammed against my ribs so hard my knees nearly buckled. Behind me, Iven cried out from the healer cot, fighting Maelin's hands as he shoved himself upright.

Iven: "He does that when he wants the hall to think for you!"
